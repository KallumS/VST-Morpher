# 0009. The pad's handle takes the accent only while held

## Context

In the house scheme the accent is for what is on. The pad's handle is a
control, like a slider's grab.

## Decision

The handle is the controls' grey with an ink rim at rest, and the accent
while you hold it, as the house slider grab is. The Edit button takes the
accent, with an ink bar under its label, while its plug-in's window is open.

## Consequences

- At rest, nothing in the window is yellow unless a plug-in window is open.
- The handle is 8.4:1 against the pad either way.
